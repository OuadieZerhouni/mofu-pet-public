Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the feedback. You were right that Mofu, Oink and Boop! were too close as separate submissions. We consolidated them: Oink, Boop! and Wuff will not be resubmitted, and their creatures now live inside this single app, rebuilt with substantial new functionality.

1. What it does / problem solved
A cozy virtual home where the user cares for a household of creatures (a fluffy Mofu, a piglet, a puppy, jelly "Boops"): a step-by-step spa (foam, rinse, towel, blow-dry) with sound and haptics, feeding each friend from its own menu, tucking them in, and a sleep-sound mixer (rain, purr, fire, ocean, chimes, noise) with a fade-out timer that keeps playing with the screen locked. It gives a calm daily ritual and a bedtime routine. Nothing decays or dies; no pressure to return.

2. Intended users
Teens and adults who like cozy games and cute characters, and people who use soothing sounds to relax or sleep. Rated 4+, not in the Kids category. Localized in 7 languages.

3. Gap addressed
Most pet apps are a single reactive character or a care loop built on decaying needs and guilt notifications. This combines a multi-creature household (friends react to each other), a hands-on ASMR grooming routine and a sleep-sound mixer in one offline, ad-free app that collects no data.

4. Beta testing
Testing was done by the developer; we have not yet run an external TestFlight group. Release builds were tested by hand room by room, the build had a UX review and a correctness review, and every change runs 300+ automated tests plus a frame-by-frame animation harness. Findings that changed this build: rapid room-arrow taps repeated the same room; a friend could land on the hatching nest; an egg hatch could get stuck when paging away; a friend moved out of the spa stayed pinned; the sleep mixer ignored the Sound switch; Share was clipped in Arabic; tapping a friend jolted its neighbours.

5. Standalone or suite?
Standalone. The account's other apps: OuO cute pet (one animated face as a screen companion), OuO Weather (forecast with a cat widget), Neko: Pixel Cat Drums & Widget (one pixel cat that drums as you tap), Ocarina Pocket (instrument), Hot Cold Globe (geography quiz), Orbit Rage (arcade game). Oink and Boop! have rejected records and will not be resubmitted. This is the only app with a multi-creature household, rooms, grooming, feeding, hatching and a sleep mixer; OuO and Neko each centre on one character's reactions.

6. Could it be an IAP or content in another app?
That applied to our earlier separate submissions, so we merged the three creature apps into this one. It does not fit inside OuO, whose scope is a single face.

7. Shared code or assets with our other apps?
Only our own in-house project template (settings storage, remote-config loader, localization setup, build scripts) and common open-source Flutter packages. The creature simulations, art, rooms, sound design and gameplay are specific to this app.

8. Shared with a third-party app?
No. All characters, illustrations and rooms were made by the developer. Some sounds were made with Stability AI's Stable Audio (credited in Settings > Credits). Only standard frameworks are used.

9. Built for a client?
No. The developer designed, built and owns the concept, content and branding. The template is our own; no commercial template or app-generation service is used.

Thank you for your time.
