Welcome to Mofu's house — a cozy little home full of cute pets who are always happy to see you.

Pet Mofu the fluffy cloud, rub Oink the piggy's belly, teach Wuff the puppy new tricks and boop the squishy little Boops. Every friend reacts to your touch in its own way, with soft, satisfying ASMR sounds.

SIX COZY ROOMS
Swipe through the living room, spa, kitchen, bedroom, playroom and garden. Bring up to four friends into a room and watch them nuzzle, pile up and play together. Shake your phone and everyone reacts.

ASMR SPA DAYS
Foam with the sponge, rinse with the warm shower, pat dry with the towel and finish with the dryer. The faster you rub, the louder the bubbles fizz. Your friend comes out shiny and happy.

SNACK TIME
Drag treats to each friend's mouth in the kitchen. Everyone has a secret favourite — find it and watch them light up.

COZY SLEEP SOUNDS
Tuck your friends in, dim the night light and mix rain, crackling fire, ocean waves, a soft purr, chimes and gentle noise. Set a sleep timer; the sounds keep playing with the screen off.

HATCH NEW FRIENDS
Caring and playing earns stars. Save enough and the egg in the garden hatches a new friend for you to name. Stars are never spent, so nothing is ever lost.

PLAY TOGETHER
Two mini-games in the playroom: boop the critters popping out of their holes, or catch falling snacks.

DRESS UP & COLLECT
New coats and colours, plus bows, scarves, hats, glasses and more as your friendships grow. Fill the journal with nearly 100 reactions, and snap pictures in the photo booth to share.

Calm, kind and gentle. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected. Just a cozy house of friends.
